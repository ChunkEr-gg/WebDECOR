# Cybercore concept artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool and checked in as WebP. The project identities and interface labels are rendered in SVG/HTML rather than baked into the artwork.

- FORME: `public/images/cybercore-forme-scan.webp` (1402 × 1122), used on the Cybercore homepage and FORME study.
- OBJECT / ROOM: `public/images/cybercore-object-wireframe.webp` (1254 × 1254), used on the Cybercore homepage and OBJECT / ROOM study.

Paths are relative to the repository root. The source artwork is served directly from `public/images/`.

## FORME — final prompt

Create a finished raster artwork for a professional experimental fashion web design concept called FORME. Use case: stylized-concept, website portfolio thumbnail. Landscape composition about 5:4. Cybercore / late-1990s digital art direction meets Swiss experimental editorial. One striking sculptural BLACK technical nylon shoulder bag, organically curved with a metal eyelet and woven strap, floating diagonally in an off-white, very pale grey flatbed-scanner field. Close crop, dramatic inky silhouette and silvery edge light. Merge high quality fashion photography with a granular photocopied halftone duplicate contour displaced slightly to the right; thin scan streaks and softly degraded raster edges. Sparse very fine black geometric contour lines and ONE small intense vermilion red cross mark, all integrated like an art-school material scan. A large soft stippled circular field sits behind the bag, a narrow black horizontal strip at the extreme top. Artful tactile image, unusual spatial tension, white space in lower left for typography added later in code. It must read as considered cybercore print artwork rather than a conventional glossy luxury product photo. Palette strictly off-white, grey, black and tiny red accent. No rendered text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no gradients, no neon glow, no circuit-board motifs, no UI screenshot, no collage of unrelated objects.

## OBJECT / ROOM — final prompt

Create a finished raster artwork for a professional experimental architecture and furniture website concept OBJECT / ROOM. Use case: stylized-concept, website portfolio thumbnail. Square composition. Late-1990s cybercore / retro-futurist architectural research sheet, polished yet tactile, mostly off-white black and charcoal. A sinuous sculptural metal lounge chair appears large and off-centre in a surreal architectural void: a black mirrored floor and enormous pale curved wall cut by a deep narrow rectangular aperture, hard photographic shadows. Half of the chair has crisp photographic metallic material, the other half resolves into an intricate fine black 3D wireframe ghost displaced laterally, like an archival CAD proof printed on paper. Above and behind is a ghosted thin line perspective grid, a subtle halftone topographic imprint, small restrained vermilion red rectangular crop markers. Deliberate asymmetric negative space in lower left for later type overlay. Contrast, geometry, subtle paper grain, printed raster artifacts. This should feel like a curated experimental cybercore design archive cover, not a standard luxurious room interior, not a generic 3D asset screenshot. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no neon glow, no blue/purple, no circuit-board pattern, no futuristic city, no UI screenshot.
