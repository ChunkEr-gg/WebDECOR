# Archived art direction: Sculptural

This is a historical concept note for a Greek-sculpture and editorial-handwriting direction. It is not part of the current site's active art direction. The generated image files mentioned in the original working notes were stored outside this repository and are not included here; no current page references them. The prompts below are retained as creative provenance only.

The original working files were named `sculptural-muse.webp` and `sculptural-fragments.webp`. They are not tracked in `public/images/`; restore or regenerate them before reusing this direction.

The archived palette was chalk white `#f8f7f4`, ink black `#1c1b1b`, soft grey, and oxblood `#7c2333`. The concept paired Bodoni Moda with Caveat. Those choices describe the archived proposal, not a guarantee about current site styling.

## Preserved generation prompts

### greekBust

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent website hero sculpture cutout. Primary request: premium Greek classical sculpture for an avant-garde handwritten editorial web-design agency website. Subject: an exquisitely carved antique white marble bust of a Greek muse, full head with finely carved wavy hair drawn back and a draped shoulder and irregular broken chest edge, head turned gently toward the viewer's left, serene face, blank carved stone eyes, very slight weathering and tiny mineral veins. Photograph a real museum-quality marble sculpture, not a living person. Composition: tall portrait, full sculpture with breathing room at edges, monumental head and shoulders, slightly off-axis three-quarter view. Lighting: directional gallery light from upper left, sculptural subtle shadows, tactile neutral white marble. BACKGROUND MUST BE GENUINELY TRANSPARENT with alpha. No plinth, no pedestal, no room, no lettering, no watermark, no gold, no flowers, no sci-fi material, no render gloss. Highly artistic and sophisticated, realism of fine-art catalogue photography.

### greekStill

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial art-studio still life for an avant-garde website. Primary request: Greek classical sculpture and artistic editorial atmosphere. Scene: pale grey handmade paper, a large weathered white plaster fragment of an ancient Greek sculpted hand resting on its side, its fingers lightly holding a single vermilion-red silk ribbon which falls in one fluid loose line across the paper. A single detached marble fragment near the back. Asymmetrical considered composition, beautiful strong negative space on the upper half. Photograph in a real contemporary sculpture studio, raking daylight from left, subtle long shadows, fine paper and stone texture, ivory, charcoal-grey, and one red ribbon. Quiet art publication quality, experimental but refined. Landscape framing 3:2. No handwriting or text inside image, no logos, no gold, no flowers, no architectural columns, no ornate props, no CGI gloss.
